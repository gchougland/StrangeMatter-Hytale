# Strange Matter anomalies: Hytale implementation

The six anomaly fields are implemented in `anomaly/AnomalyService.java` and `HytaleAnomalyEffects.java`. The service owns persistent field identities; Hytale entities are temporary render anchors. Removing a render anchor, unloading a chunk, or restarting the server does not remove an anomaly. Anchors are intangible, invulnerable, and explicitly nonserialized. Each field has a bespoke model, a layered finite particle system renewed once a second, and the original Strange Matter ambient recording played spatially at its measured clip duration.

## Gameplay mapping

| Field | Minecraft behavior used as source | Hytale behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gravity | Eight block field; distance-weighted low gravity; drifting items and NPCs; creative players exempt | Server velocity instructions deliver lift to client-controlled players; NPCs/items receive the source's damped, phase-offset float/drift pattern. Effects cease on exit without changing movement defaults. |
| Temporal Bloom | Eight block field; crops randomly gain or lose one or two growth stages every five seconds; animal age flips every ten seconds | Native `FarmingBlock` stage progression and stage application change the crop, including mature crops whose block entity was removed. Supported livestock change between actual adult/juvenile NPC roles, preserving UUID and tamed variants. Animals made young mature after twenty loaded minutes, matching Minecraft's `setAge(-24000)`. Other NPCs receive a temporal burst. |
| Energetic Rift | Six block zap; one point out of twenty HP every two seconds; a rod within eight blocks suppresses all entity zaps; grounded discharge every ten seconds; tinfoil hat protection | Five damage against Hytale's usual 100 HP baseline, through the declared `SM_Energetic_Rift` elemental damage cause and native damage events/resistances. Hytale has no native `Lightning` cause; the earlier fallback incorrectly treated discharges as Physical damage. Lightning rods and enabled registered Rift Stabilizers ground it. A disabled stabilizer block cannot independently ground a field. Discharge hooks expose a power source to machines. A worn Tinfoil Hat protects the wearer; Creative players are exempt. Global native invulnerability and incoming-damage rules still apply. |
| Warp Gate | Two block activation; distant paired gates, usually 1,000–5,000 blocks away; five second teleport lockout | Persistent symmetric UUID pairs. A distant partner is generated asynchronously when an unpaired gate is entered. Arrival candidates four to six blocks outside the gate must fit the complete entity collider, have loaded terrain, dry feet/body space, and no damaging blocks. A per-entity cooldown prevents immediate return and allows several players to use a gate. Capturing either end temporarily disables the link. |
| Echoing Shadow | Eight block darkness field; 25% spawn attempt per second; at most six tracked hostile mobs; protect undead from sunlight | A black particle volume, entity tint, and a field spawn pool of Hytale zombies, skeleton fighters/archers, and spiders. At most six tracked mobs per field. Two-second fire protection and extinguishing refresh while inside. Unloaded tracked mobs retain their population slot. |
| Thoughtwell | Six block field; five-to-ten-second player nausea; tinfoil protection; five-second false mob appearances every ten seconds; 30% chance to forget a target | Native screen overlay, tint, mild movement disorientation, a distance-weighted duration, temporary Hytale mob model swaps, and a 30% locked-target reset. A worn Tinfoil Hat blocks cognition effects. Original NPC identity, behavior, drops, and persisted model remain intact. |

The source files used are the six `entity/*Entity.java` anomaly classes, `BaseAnomalyEntity`, `Config.java`, `worldgen/WorldGenUtils.java`, `AnomalyUtil.java`, scanner/research registration, and the source ambient sounds. Constants are converted from 20 Minecraft ticks per second into seconds; Hytale tick rate does not affect field cadence.

## Native adaptations and current limits

Hytale has no universal Minecraft-style animal `Age` attribute in the inspected API. Temporal Bloom therefore uses the supported `RoleChangeSystem.requestRoleChange` path for fifteen known livestock families, including `Tamed_` variants. This rebuilds the same UUID/holder as its adult or juvenile role, changing native behavior, appearance, and role-specific health/drop configuration. It is a real persisted transformation. A separate persisted maturation clock counts only loaded simulation time and returns an animal made young to its matching adult role after 1,200 seconds. Taming performed while the animal is young is preserved during maturation. This is Hytale's role-based age equivalent; Minecraft breeding internals are not reproduced.

Thoughtwell disguises intentionally remain appearance-only, preserving the source's deceptive behavior rather than changing the mob's real identity, AI, or drops. They expire after five seconds, never overwrite `PersistentModel`, restore on world shutdown, and reload as the true persisted model. Another plugin's later model change takes precedence over restoring ours.

Thoughtwell's visual disorientation uses a native screen effect and small movement penalty, rather than Minecraft's oscillating nausea shader. Echoing Shadow uses the opaque particle field rather than editing the world's lighting array. These effects use public server assets and ECS APIs; no Noesis or client injection is assumed.

Ambient clips are finite spatial sounds, not unbounded client loops. Capturing an anomaly stops new sound and particle emission immediately; an already playing clip can finish (four seconds for most fields, 6.075 seconds for Gravity). Model and particle art are recreated by the asset pipeline; the six original OGG recordings are retained.

## Generation and resources

New chunks receive a deterministic, seed-based population pass in `ChunkPreLoadProcessEvent`, before their terrain can be edited by players. Existing chunks receive field discovery within four 16-block cells of each player every two seconds, with no terrain changes. Every cell is recorded once. Hytale chunks are larger than Minecraft chunks, so the source probability is applied per 16×16 footprint: **1/500 per anomaly type**; six types are six independent rolls, not a single 1/500 roll shared among them. Water, lava, insufficient headroom, leaves, and nearby existing anomalies reject a spawn. This does not replace the selected Hytale world generator or keep distant chunks force-loaded.

The original configuration defaults to uniform biome eligibility. This port preserves that default and supports per-type, per-world, and per-Hytale-environment rarity overrides. Copy `Server/StrangeMatter/Anomalies/generation-defaults.json` from the JAR to the plugin data directory as `anomaly-generation.json` to customize it. Keys in `typeRarity` are enum names such as `GRAVITY`; `worldRarity` and `environmentRarity` contain maps of those keys. An override of zero disables that type in the selected world/environment. Generated instance worlds are excluded, and `excludedWorlds` can exclude additional exact world names.

Fields selected during new-chunk generation convert every raw native soil layer in their patch: exposed sand, clay, gravel, mud, snow, ash or dirt becomes anomalous grass, and buried natural soil becomes anomalous dirt. Type-specific shard ore plus resonite generate underneath. The source column probabilities are retained: 25% resonite, 10% anomaly shard, one to three blocks per successful column. At the user's request, each enabled deposit type also gets a minimum vein when suitable geology exists, so unlucky rolls cannot produce only resonite. A zero chance still disables its resource. Ore searching follows the source down to the world bottom rather than stopping after 24 blocks; all raw Hytale rock families qualify, including desert Sandstone, Lime, Shale, Volcanic, Calcite and natural mossy/cracked variants. This broadens the eligible host types while retaining the original vein density. Shaped blocks, existing ores and bedrock are excluded. These edits write only the newly generated raw chunk holder. Patches never cross into an adjacent existing chunk; therefore a patch near a chunk edge may be clipped. Existing chunks and player-placed terrain are never rewritten, even when made from ordinary stone or dirt. Fields discovered retroactively, and out-of-band distant gate partners, do not add deposits. Warp gate patches have radius five; other patches have radius four. `terrainPatches=false` disables even the fresh-generation patches. Ordinary global resonite ore generation is separate from these anomaly deposits.

## Integration API

Construct `AnomalyService(Path pluginDataDirectory)`. Call `tick(World, double dtSeconds)` on the world's execution queue, outside active store iteration, because core/NPC/block-entity creation changes archetypes. Call `stopWorld(World)` before world shutdown to restore model overrides, remove anchors, and flush the save. Calling `save()` periodically or at plugin shutdown is safe; it only writes dirty state.

Register `onChunkPreLoad(ChunkPreLoadProcessEvent)` as a global event handler during setup. Its newly-generated guard is the authorization boundary for terrain edits; the normal tick/discovery paths do not edit terrain. The source's first-contact advancement is represented by a persisted per-player observed-discipline set. `setFirstContactHook((player, anomaly, researchAward) -> ...)` lets a journal display first exposure. `researchAward` defaults to zero, since the Minecraft effect trigger awards no research points. A server may explicitly opt into an award with `setFirstContactResearchAward`; repeat exposure is deduplicated.

`nearest(world, position, radius[, type])` supports resonators and machine probes. `inView(world, eye, direction, range)` supports scanner targeting even when the core has no collision box. The scanner should call research progression only when `AnomalyRecord.scannable()` is true, using `"anomaly:" + record.id` as its persistent scan key and `record.type.researchType` with an amount of ten. Capsule-released anomalies are permanently marked released and cannot mint repeat scan rewards.

`capture(UUID)` returns a `CapturedAnomaly` with `itemId()` and an opaque `token()`. Persist that token on the item. `release(token, world, position)` validates its identity and one-use nonce before restoring the same field. Copying a filled item does not duplicate a field. If giving the capsule fails, `cancelCapture(token)` rolls back the exact capture without changing original scan provenance. Capsule release positions must be finite and within build height. Inventory mutations and field operations should be treated as one world-thread transaction.

`pair(first, second)` replaces old links symmetrically. `setGroundingHook`, `setDischargeHook`, and `setSuppressionHook` connect Rift Stabilizers, energy harvesting, and stasis machinery. Disabling a field suppresses its runtime effects and render anchor while retaining its identity.

## Verification

`AnomalyVerification.run()` exercises natural scan eligibility, persisted capture, rejection of duplicate captures, exact-token transaction rollback, stale-token rejection after recapture, unique nonces, type naming, environment rarity isolation, first-contact deduplication/default economy, native adult/juvenile role names, taming preservation, and the twenty-minute maturation default. It runs without a live server. All anomaly Java code compiles against the installed September 7 Hytale server JAR. Crop stage behavior, networked player lift, particle layering, NPC role rebuilds, and asynchronous travel still require a live multiplayer playtest; compilation and state tests do not establish visual or movement quality on a connected Hytale client.

The September 9 native revision harness passed against the installed server: a real Cow lost exactly five health to `SM_Energetic_Rift`, the native Invulnerable component cancelled the same discharge, and a stabilizer block alone did not bypass the enabled grounding hook. All six anomaly types produced both resonite and their matching shard beneath a 30-block-deep gap when every density roll was forced to miss; shaped stone and bedrock were preserved, and zero ore probabilities disabled the guarantees. These checks run native loaded assets, chunks and damage systems. They do not simulate a connected player or assert client rendering quality.

Native server run `20260909-015909-465-6c329b36` passed `NATIVE_TERRAIN_HOST_VERIFICATION_PASSED`: six soil strata, sandstone mixed deposits, original vein density, protected geology, existing-chunk rejection and chunk-edge clipping.
