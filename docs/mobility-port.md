# Hoverboard and Echoform Imprinter

`MobilityTools` implements the hoverboard as a native Hytale NPC mount using the complete native horse `Mount` movement settings: steering, sprint boost, jumping, inertia and collision remain under the rider's native controller, with flight disabled. The custom model is displayed at 2x scale, with a 1.1-block lower collision skirt and rider anchor at 0.54. The board spawns with its collider's bottom at the player's feet and deploys only near ground. Deployment consumes the actual board item after two required native inventory save acknowledgements. Dismounting, death, disconnect, world transfer or server/world shutdown removes the disposable entity and queues the intact item for return. Full inventory leaves a persistent receipt waiting for a free slot. All durability, custom metadata and other native item fields survive; a private identity nonce prevents older saved copies from deploying another board. The rider's prior movement settings are restored only in the original world; destination join settings and world-local mount IDs are left to the destination's native lifecycle. This replaces Minecraft's separately parked entity/pickup interaction; water-surface hovering and Minecraft's exact hover spring have not been reproduced.

The board carries native `Frozen` without `StepComponent` to suspend only NPC simulation. Hytale's `GamePacketHandler.handleMountMovement` still accepts rider positions and movement flags. This resolves the downhill conflict in which the NPC Walk steering system applied gravity/collision after the client had already moved the board down a step. The entity remains a native mount; neither rider movement nor its client controller is frozen. Native dismount can rebuild an NPC holder with a new entity reference, so cleanup also resolves the stable entity UUID to prevent a leftover board.

`hoverboards.json` keeps full native BSON item payloads and phases `PREPARING`, `RESERVED`, `MOUNTED`, `RETURNING` and `AVAILABLE`. Preparation stamps/saves identity while the physical item is still present. Only its acknowledgement permits exact native inventory removal; the removal save must also finish before mounting. Restarting preparation never invents an item. Restarting reserved/mounted state rotates the nonce and reconciles a queued return against old inventory copies. Returns search backpack, storage and hotbar and require a completed native player save before the receipt becomes available again. Save failures retain and retry the receipt; shutdown never blocks its world thread waiting for disk. A player inventory and this mod's ledger cannot form one cross-file atomic transaction under abrupt process/power failure; durable intent, native per-player ordered saves and exclusive nonce validation ensure stale copies cannot create two usable boards.

The Echoform Imprinter retains its one-second charged scan, five-block range, aim requirement and crouch-to-revert control. The caller sends `imprint_start`, `imprint_complete` or `cancel` through the native charging interaction; an unstarted, premature, cancelled or out-of-range completion cannot apply a morph. Target life, line of sight, held equipment and target identity are checked again at completion.

The imprinter copies a creature model or another player's appearance using native `ModelComponent` and `PlayerSkinComponent`. It preserves the user's original collision box, view height and physical parameters, matching the original cosmetic disguise. The original model and skin are restored on crouch-use, death, or world change. The implementation resets Hytale's automatically rewritten `PersistentModel` reference immediately after the temporary disguise is applied, so the disguise cannot become the player's permanently saved model. An appearance replacement by another mod takes precedence over restoring this temporary disguise.
